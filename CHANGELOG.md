## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-24

### Added
- Control the VG-X4 straight from Chrome or Edge over USB: connect, move the pen, set the origin and start position, save positions and define the travel area
- Preview every drawing before plotting, play the pen path stroke by stroke, resume from any stroke, change the drawing speed live and follow the progress in the bottom bar
- Write text with single-line fonts on paper, folded cards, place cards and Swiss C6/5 and C5 envelopes, and fill in addresses from a CSV list
- Card designer with up to four pens per element, rotation, a freehand pen, an eraser, and undo and redo
- Turn photos and images into line drawings with outlines, hatching, smooth curves or one continuous line, and import SVGs with each colour as its own pen
- Create AI drawings with your own API key, including multicoloured drawings and photos redrawn as simple line art
- Design your own handwriting font with a printable template you can plot, fill in and scan
- Save drawings in a gallery, download them as SVG or image, and back up all settings automatically to a folder such as iCloud Drive
- English and German interface with a dark mode that follows your system setting
