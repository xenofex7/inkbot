# Third-party notices

inkbot bundles single-line fonts in `fonts/` (SVG sources) and `src/lib/text/fonts.json` (converted with `npm run fonts`).

## Hershey fonts

Hershey Script 1-stroke, Hershey Sans 1-stroke, Hershey Script medium, Hershey Serif medium italic, Hershey Gothic German and Hershey Gothic English, SVG conversion by Windell H. Oskay (Evil Mad Scientist Laboratories), adapted from emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey via "Hershey Fonts in SVG" by Marty McGuire.

- The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
- The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821 (mit-eddie!ci-dandelion!hurt).

The full use restriction is part of the metadata in each `fonts/Hershey*.svg` file.
