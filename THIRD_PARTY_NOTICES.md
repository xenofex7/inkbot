# Third-party notices

inkbot bundles single-line fonts in `fonts/` (SVG sources) and `src/lib/text/fonts.json` (converted with `npm run fonts`).

- The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
- The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821 (mit-eddie!ci-dandelion!hurt).

The full use restriction is part of the metadata in `fonts/HersheyScript1.svg` and `fonts/HersheySans1.svg`.
