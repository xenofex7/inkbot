Brand palette · Steel

Steel blue from the robot's tin body. Steel-600 carries links and the focus ring on light surfaces, Steel-400 is decorative (scrollbar, focus ring on dark), Steel-200 takes links on dark, Steel-900 (night ink) is the dark theme canvas and footer. Mid stops 300/500/700 are left out on purpose.

Typography

System typeface in two roles, no webfont: Display (600 with negative letter spacing) for headlines, Body (400-600) for text, buttons and navigation. SF Pro on Apple devices, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android. Display weight stays 600 across all sizes, never 700. A brand face (e.g. Inter, Manrope, Geist) is self-hosted and keeps the weight and tracking signature.
